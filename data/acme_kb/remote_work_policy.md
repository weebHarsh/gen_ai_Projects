# Hybrid & Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-004)
Effective: 1 January 2026.

- The standard model is **hybrid: 3 days per week in office** (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday are core days).
- Fully remote work is allowed for up to **15 working days per year** with manager approval.
- Employees working remotely must use the company VPN (GlobalConnect) for all internal systems.
- A one-time home-office allowance of **INR 15,000** is reimbursed on submission of receipts within 60 days of joining.
- Working from another country requires HR and Legal approval at least **30 days** in advance.
