# IT Security Policy (IT-POL-001)
Effective: 1 February 2026. Owner: Security Team.

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** and are rotated every **180 days**.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, GitHub and the HR portal.
- Laptops lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity; full-disk encryption is required.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@acme.example within **1 hour**.
- Customer data must never be pasted into public AI tools. Approved AI tools: the internal "AcmeGPT" assistant and Claude via the company workspace.
- Phishing reports go to the "Report Phish" button in Outlook; do not forward suspicious emails.
