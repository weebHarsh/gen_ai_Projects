# New Joiner Onboarding (HR-GUIDE-003)

- **Day 1**: laptop collection at 9:30 AM from IT desk, Noida office, 4th floor.
- **Week 1**: complete mandatory trainings: Information Security, POSH, Code of Conduct (deadline: 7 days from joining).
- Probation period is **6 months**; notice period during probation is **15 days**, after confirmation **60 days**.
- Each joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy for the first 90 days.
- Payroll cut-off is the **20th** of each month; joiners after the 20th are paid in the next cycle.
