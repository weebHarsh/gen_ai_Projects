# Leave Policy (HR-POL-001)
Effective: 1 April 2026. Owner: People Team.

## Annual leave
- Full-time employees get **24 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month.
- Up to **8 unused days** can be carried forward to the next year; the rest lapse on 31 March.
- Leave of 5 or more consecutive days must be requested at least **14 days** in advance.

## Sick leave
- **12 days** of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's certificate is required for absences longer than **3 consecutive days**.
- Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Parental leave
- Primary caregivers: **26 weeks** paid. Secondary caregivers: **4 weeks** paid.
- Must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Public holidays
Acme observes 12 public holidays per year; the list is published each December on the intranet.
