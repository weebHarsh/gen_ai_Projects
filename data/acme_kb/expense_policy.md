# Travel & Expense Policy (FIN-POL-002)
Effective: 1 April 2026. Owner: Finance.

## Submission
- Expenses must be submitted in the Expensify tool within **30 days** of being incurred.
- Any single expense above **INR 5,000** needs an itemised receipt.
- Claims above **INR 50,000** need approval from a Director in addition to the line manager.

## Travel
- Domestic flights: economy class only. International flights over **8 hours**: premium economy allowed.
- Hotel cap: **INR 7,000 per night** in metro cities, INR 4,500 elsewhere.
- Daily meal allowance on travel: **INR 1,500** (domestic), USD 60 (international).

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades paid for personally.
